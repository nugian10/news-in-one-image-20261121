One image that sums up the world's news of Saturday 21 November 2026, set as a newspaper front page. The lead story is the magnitude 6.8 earthquake that struck the Andes of central Peru near Junín and was felt strongly in Lima; below it, the COP31 climate summit in Antalya runs into overtime over fossil fuels, and Iran's foreign minister Araqchi seeks diplomacy while President Trump rejects Tehran's terms. A brief box carries India's record ₹1.22 lakh crore foreign inflow and Cyclone Akvan's landfall on the Odisha coast, and a ticker along the bottom rounds up the day's other stories, from the deadly storm in southern Brazil to the magnitude 5.7 quake in Kumamoto, Japan.

The front page is authored in HTML (`frontpage.html`, no external assets) and rendered to `entry.png` at 1024×1024; `main.py` copies that image to the output directory and verifies its sha256.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
