Peru · Earthquake

6.8 QUAKE ROCKS
THE ANDES

A magnitude 6.8 earthquake struck the Andes of central Peru, near Junín, on Friday evening. It was felt strongly in Lima, where residents ran into the streets and lawmakers walked out of Congress. No casualties or significant damage were reported — and no tsunami warning was issued.

Climate · Antalya

Climate summit deadlocked, runs into overtime

The COP31 talks in Turkey ran past their deadline as nations deadlocked over fossil fuels. The EU warned the summit risks becoming a “fossil summit”. Negotiators stayed at the table past the scheduled close, still searching for a deal.

Iran · Diplomacy

Tehran seeks talks; Trump rejects its terms

Foreign minister Araqchi says Iran wants diplomacy to end the war, but President Trump calls its terms unacceptable. Iran is moving to restart oil exports despite the US naval blockade.
